## Featured Topics

### The Questions That Matter

Public sector organizations face challenges that are rarely discussed openly. We believe asking better questions leads to better outcomes:

- **On Risk:** How many organizations have enterprise-wide visibility into risk across all silos? How are risk appetite decisions being made—through quantified analysis or default risk-elimination?

- **On Resources:** Can your organization factually demonstrate effective resource utilization? What evidence exists beyond "we do a good job"?

- **On Investment:** Are capital decisions driven by integrated lifecycle data or primarily by asset age? Do documented triggers exist for refurbishment versus replacement?

- **On Operations:** Would emerging issues be detected before they impact service? Is your operational culture reactive or proactive?

These aren't criticisms—they're starting points for meaningful improvement.

## Practical Guides

### Self-Assessment Questions

Use these questions to evaluate your organization's current state:

**Enterprise Risk Management**
1. Does your organization maintain a documented enterprise risk register?
2. Is risk visibility limited to individual departments or available across the organization?
3. When did leadership last discuss organizational risk appetite in relation to cost and service levels?
4. Are mitigation decisions based on quantified analysis or primarily on eliminating risk regardless of cost?

**Resource Utilization**
1. What metrics does your organization use to measure workforce productivity?
2. Can you provide factual evidence of effective resource utilization to stakeholders?
3. How does your organization identify low-value activities that could be redirected?
4. Are work management processes documented and consistently followed?

**Capital Investment**
1. Are capital investment triggers based on asset age alone or integrated lifecycle data?
2. Does your organization have documented criteria for refurbishment versus replacement decisions?
3. How is condition and performance data incorporated into capital prioritization?
4. Can you demonstrate that capital dollars flow to interventions with the greatest community value?

**Operational Resilience**
1. Would your organization detect an emerging issue before service is impacted?
2. Are monitoring and alarm systems providing actionable early warning?
3. How quickly can your organization respond to an unplanned infrastructure event?
4. Would you characterize your operational culture as primarily reactive or proactive?

*If these questions reveal gaps, [our micro-consulting services](/services/micro-consulting/) can help you develop a path forward.*
