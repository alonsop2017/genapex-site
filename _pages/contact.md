## What to Expect

When you reach out, here's what happens:

1. **We'll acknowledge your inquiry** within one business day
2. **We'll schedule a brief call** to understand your situation (no cost, no obligation)
3. **We'll provide honest feedback** on whether and how we can help
4. **You decide** what makes sense for your organization

We're not interested in selling services you don't need. We'd rather have a good conversation that doesn't lead to an engagement than push something that isn't the right fit.

---

## Common Starting Points

People typically reach out when they're:

- **Questioning current approaches** to risk, capital investment, or resource management
- **Preparing for a significant decision** and want an independent perspective
- **Looking to demonstrate value** to council, boards, or other stakeholders
- **Exploring what "better" could look like** in their operations
- **Seeking practical guidance** on a specific challenge

If any of these resonate, we should talk.
